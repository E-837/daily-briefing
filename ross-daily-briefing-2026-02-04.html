CloudX Hits GA — MoPub's Spiritual Successor, Now With AI Agents

The co-founders of MoPub (and MAX) are back. CloudX launched into general availability with a pitch: let AI agents handle the grunt work of mobile ad monetization — tweaking floors, managing line items, adjusting demand paths in real time. CEO Jim Payne says the bottleneck has always been what a single ad ops person can tactically do. CloudX deploys specialized agents for SDK integrations, ad format setup, and ongoing optimization. Been in beta with publishers since Q4.

Amazon Ads Opens Beta for Its MCP Server

Amazon Ads is betting on Model Context Protocol as the connective tissue between agentic AI systems and its ad platform. The MCP server acts as a "translation layer" so agents don't have to wrestle with granular APIs directly — think natural language prompts that spin up full campaigns with ad groups, audiences, and budgets. VP Paula Despins says the problem they saw internally was agents going rogue (e.g., analyzing 3 years of AMC data instead of just reading the standard report). MCP gives agents guardrails.

Prebid Takes Over AdCP Code for Sell-Side AI Agents

Prebid is now the home for publisher-side AI agents built on Ad Context Protocol. The "Prebid Sales Agent" is open-source on GitHub and available to all publishers. The vision: specialized AI agents on both buy and sell sides negotiating programmatic deals autonomously. Magnite's Garrett McGrath (Prebid board chair) says Prebid's publisher-first DNA makes it the right steward. AgenticAdvertising.org (AAO) — yet another acronym — governs the standard.

→ Read full article

IAB Launches "Project Eidos" to Unify Measurement

Not new tech, but new structure. The IAB is consolidating its scattered measurement initiatives under one umbrella called Project Eidos (Greek for "to see"). VP Angelina Eng says the industry has been relying on "digital duct tape" — and even basic definitions remain unsettled. Is a video podcast a video ad or a podcast ad? Is a smart TV homepage tile CTV? Later this year, Eidos will standardize data inputs feeding into MMM products. The IAB estimates the lack of standards costs the industry significant waste.

🎙️ The Big Story: "Guess It's AdsGPT Now?"

OpenAI announced it's testing ads in ChatGPT search — after Sam Altman called ads a "last resort" just two years ago at Harvard. Omnicom Digital CEO Jonathan Nelson says agencies need to help brands weigh jumping in early vs. dealing with growing pains (remember Netflix's insane launch CPMs?). Nelson's take: the gap between Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and OpenAI launching ads will look tiny in hindsight.
