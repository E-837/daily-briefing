📉 People Inc. Says Who Needs Google?
The Editor's Choice story. People Inc. (formerly Dotdash Meredith) lost 50% of its Google search traffic over the last two years — down from 70% of total traffic five years ago to just 30% today. Zero-click AI search is the culprit.

But here's the twist: Digital revenue grew 14% YoY in Q4 ($355M) — highest growth in five quarters. How?

  • Off-platform pivot — mobile apps, Instagram, TikTok, Apple News integrations

  • Off-platform views nearly doubled in 2 years, up 43% YoY in Q4

  • Non-session revenue now 38% of total digital revenue ($137M, up 37% YoY)

  • D/Cipher contextual targeting platform expanding to third-party publishers and CTV

CEO Neil Vogel: "This is the model for our future — strong growth from non-session-based revenue while absorbing continued declines in Google referral traffic."

🏛️ IAB CEO David Cohen: "Free Riding Is Stealing"
At IAB's Annual Leadership Meeting, Cohen announced the Accountability for Publishers Act — proposed legislation requiring AI companies to pay publishers for training content. Two senators' offices already interested.

Key quotes:

  • "Every single company training AI without compensating publishers is free riding. Free riding isn't just unfair. It's stealing."

  • "The health of the publisher community is critical to the health of our overall business. If we don't have publishers, we don't have content."

Also noted: Pay TV fell below 50% of US households, which will affect local TV and upfronts.

🤖 When Does AI Help vs. Hurt Marketing? Airpost Raises $4.1M
New AI video ad platform Airpost raised seed funding with a contrarian pitch: AI alone "can't be creative and get high-performing results."

Their hybrid model:

  • Brands upload assets and guidelines

  • AI generates creative brief (human-revisable)

  • Creative team assembles final product using mix of AI-generated + organic content

  • Minimum 10 unique ads/week per client + unlimited versioning

Example: AI wrote a deodorant ad line that sounded "a little AI-ish." Human revised it from generic copy to "I was tired of smelling like a scented candle" — drove better performance.

CEO John Gargiulo: "If your company wants to lay off half its employees and solely use AI-generated content, we're not for you."

⚖️ MRC Releases Auction Transparency Standards
The Media Rating Council dropped new standards for digital ad auction transparency with a voluntary certification. Driven by Omnicom's Ben Hovaness, who's been educating agencies on auction theory since 2014.

Requirements for certification:

  • Disclose auction type (first-price, second-price, modified)

  • Reveal factors beyond bid price that influence results

  • Share reserve price/floor practices

  • Apply same floors to all buyers (no preferential treatment)

  • Disclose all "key decision variables" and rule changes

70 companies involved including Meta, TikTok, X, The Trade Desk, Hearst, and the 4A's/ANA/IAB Tech Lab.

Hovaness: "If you go to Sotheby's or Christie's, you get a term sheet at the door. That is how you run a good auction with high integrity."
